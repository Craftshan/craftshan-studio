↳ who we are

More than a shop — a design journey built through practice

Craftshan Studio is the personal creative work of M Kashian Malik, founded around a simple belief: good digital design should look clean, feel thoughtful, and be practical for the person who is going to make something with it. What began with curiosity and a willingness to learn gradually became a focused direction in laser-cut and crafting design.

The story behind Craftshan

The name Craftshan combines “Craft”, reflecting the world of crafting and making, with “Shan”, taken from Kashian’s name. It represents both sides of the journey — the love of making useful designs and the personal effort behind building a creative identity.

While he was a computer science student, Kashian was also steadily developing his design skills outside his studies. The early stage was far from ideal. He worked on a low-end laptop where even opening Adobe Illustrator could take several minutes. Instead of waiting for better equipment, he chose to work with what he had: learning the software, practising, studying references, testing ideas, and improving one design at a time.

With continuous support from his father, his setup eventually improved and gave him more room to experiment. Another important influence was his close friend Saqib Ahmed, whom he considers like an elder brother. Saqib introduced him to the foundations of design, helped him through early challenges, and encouraged him to keep developing the skill instead of treating it as a temporary hobby.

Designed for real makers

That experience shaped the way Craftshan Studio approaches digital design today. The focus is on practical laser-cut files such as ornaments, earrings, keychains, signs, layered pieces, and other maker-friendly projects. The goal is not to make a design complicated for the sake of looking impressive. The goal is to make it clear, balanced, usable, and ready for the creative process that comes after the download.

Each design begins with an idea, but the details matter just as much. Cut lines, shapes, spacing, layers, proportions, and the overall ease of use all influence whether a file is genuinely useful. Kashian believes that one of the most valuable skills in design is the ability to think of new possibilities and then turn those ideas into something people can actually make.

A studio built on consistency

Craftshan Studio is being built step by step. Seasonal demand, new crafting trends, and the needs of makers all help shape what gets designed next, while quality and usefulness remain at the centre of the work. The aim is to grow a catalog that customers can return to when they need a clean, dependable file for their next project.

There is also a strong belief in listening to customers. When something can be improved, the right response is to learn from the feedback and make it better. Long-term trust matters more than a quick sale, because a digital product store becomes meaningful when people know that the person behind it genuinely cares about the work.

Where Craftshan Studio is going

The long-term goal is simple: continue building a creative career through skill, consistency, and useful design. Craftshan Studio is not presented as a large company or a marketplace of many creators. It is one independent studio, one founder, and a growing body of work shaped by years of learning, experimentation, and a genuine interest in making better files for makers.

Every design is another step in that journey — from an early idea on a screen to a finished physical project in someone’s hands. That is what Craftshan Studio is here to support.

Once a digital product has been made available to you, Craftshan Studio generally does not provide refunds simply because you changed your mind, no longer need the design, or prefer a different product. Please review the product images, description, included formats, compatibility information, and license before purchasing.

If there is a genuine problem with a purchased design, we will first try to help you resolve it. Depending on the situation, we may provide a corrected file, replacement download, or another reasonable solution.

  • An identical product was purchased twice and the duplicate can be verified.
  • The delivered product is materially different from the listing.

If something is not working correctly, contact Craftshan Studio as soon as possible. Please provide your order number, product name, a short explanation of the problem, and—when useful—a screenshot or error message.

Laser machines, cutting software, operating systems, materials, and workflows vary widely. Craftshan Studio provides the formats stated on the product page but cannot guarantee compatibility with every machine or software configuration.

A refund does not create a right to continue selling, producing, sharing, or distributing work based on the refunded digital design.

Craftshan Studio may decline requests that reasonably appear to involve repeated refund abuse, false technical claims, attempts to obtain files without payment, fraudulent chargebacks, or continued use of a product after a refund.

10. Chargebacks & Payment Disputes

If you believe there is a billing or delivery problem, please contact Craftshan Studio before starting a chargeback whenever reasonably possible. We will make reasonable efforts to investigate legitimate issues and provide an appropriate resolution.

11. Mandatory Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove or restrict any consumer protection, cancellation right, refund right, replacement right, or other remedy that applicable law does not permit us to exclude.

For a support request, use the Craftshan Studio contact page and include your order number, product name, and a clear description of the issue.

Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy explains how Craftshan Studio, an independent digital design store operated by M Kashian Malik, uses cookies and similar browser storage when you visit craftshan.studio, and the choices you have. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies?

Cookies are small text files that a website can save on your device. Craftshan Studio mostly uses a close relative called browser storage (“local storage”). It works in a similar way, but the information stays in your browser instead of being sent to our servers with every request.

In this policy, “cookies” means both cookies and this kind of browser storage.

2. The Short Version

  • We only use what the shop needs to work: your login, your cart and a few preferences.
  • We do not currently run analytics or advertising trackers, and we do not show third-party ads.
  • Optional categories stay off unless you switch them on.
  • You can change your choice at any time using “Cookie preferences” in the footer.

3. Essential Storage

These items are needed to run the shop, so they are always on. They are not used for advertising.

  • Sign-in session — keeps you signed in after you log in with your email code. It is stored by our authentication provider and removed when you sign out or clear your browser data.
  • Cart and coupon craftshan_cart craftshan_applied_coupon — remember the designs in your cart and any discount code you applied.
  • Account details cache craftshan_login_state craftshanCustomerProfile — lets your account page show your name and sign-in status quickly.
  • Store settings cache craftshan_storefront_cache craftshanGlobalSettings craftshan_maintenance_cache — help pages load faster and let us show a maintenance notice when needed.
  • Your cookie choice craftshan_cookie_consent — remembers what you picked so we do not ask again on every visit.

4. Preference Storage

We remember your display choice so the website looks the way you left it.

  • Theme craftshan_theme — stores whether you chose the dark or the light look.

5. Analytics

Craftshan Studio does not currently use analytics tools. If we add them in the future, we will update this policy first, and where the law requires consent they will load only if you have turned on Analytics in Cookie preferences.

6. Advertising and Marketing

We do not use advertising cookies, social media tracking pixels, or retargeting tools. If that ever changes, they will stay off until you allow them.

7. Accounts and Signing In

You can browse the shop without an account. An account is needed for some actions, such as unlocking downloads or sending us a message so we can reply to your email. When you sign in, a session is saved in your browser so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it.

8. Payments

Paid checkout is handled by Lemon Squeezy on its own secure page. Lemon Squeezy may set its own cookies to process the payment, prevent fraud, and meet legal requirements. We do not control those cookies, so please see Lemon Squeezy’s own policies for details.

9. Third-Party Services

Some parts of the website are provided by other companies. When your browser loads them, they can receive technical information that any website visit shares, such as your IP address and browser type.

  • Supabase — provides sign-in, our database, and file storage.
  • Google Fonts — Google serves the fonts used on the website.
  • jsDelivr — a content delivery network that serves a software library the website needs.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the website and may keep standard server logs.
  • Lemon Squeezy — processes paid checkout (see section 8).

These providers are governed by their own privacy and cookie policies.

10. Links to Other Websites

Our footer links to our profiles on Pinterest, Atomm, Creative Fabrica, Fiverr, Instagram, and Facebook. Once you click through, you are on their website and their own cookies and policies apply.

11. Your Choices

The first time you visit, you can choose:

  • Accept all — allow optional analytics and marketing, if we ever use them.
  • Essential only — allow only the storage the shop needs to work.
